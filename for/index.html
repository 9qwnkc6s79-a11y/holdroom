Who it’s for

Firms that will not give the file to a lab.

If the document is the franchise, it should not cross the building line. These are the buyers we are building for now.

Law firms

Privilege and the paste problem

Associates already use frontier chat. The risk is the memo, the transcript, the draft that should never have left counsel’s systems. Holdroom is a place to ask that lives on your floor.

We are not a law firm and do not give legal advice. Privilege is your policy. The box is how you stop the easy leak.

PE / deal shops

The firm is the data

IC memos, models, and portfolio files are the product. Cloud chat is fine for public news. It is the wrong room for the deck that moves a deal.

Size the box by how many people ask in a busy hour, not by AUM. Hold M is the default for a 5–10 person shop.

Operators

Pricing, source, customers

If you will not upload the spreadsheet, you should not have to choose between no AI and someone else’s cloud. Drop the file on hardware you own.

This is for real IP — not a generic chatbot for the website.

Not yet

Medical later.

Clinical operators may be a later fit. Paperwork comes first. This site makes no HIPAA claim and no treatment claim. If that is you, say so — we will not pretend we are ready.

Not a fit

If you need the smartest model on earth.

Use a frontier lab for that. Holdroom is for work you will not paste. Most serious firms should keep both until they trust the box.
