02 Who it’s for

Firms that will not give the file to a lab.

If the document is the franchise, it should stay in the room — at the desk and on the road. These are buyer situations, not case studies. No logos. No invented customers.

Law

Privilege is a paste away.

Tuesday, 11:40 p.m. The associate has the memo. Frontier chat is a tab. Privilege is one paste. The lab will process the text even if they promised not to train on it.

What they need is an in-house suite on the firm’s network, customized to counsel’s documents, with seats the owner controls. The memo stays with the firm. It does not go to a data company because the deadline is morning.

They also leave the office. A hearing trip, a phone in a hallway. The same private system should be reachable — not a second paste into a lab because they are not at the machine. We are not a law firm and do not give legal advice. Privilege is your policy.

PE / deal shops

The IC deck is the firm.

Sunday. The model and the deck are the franchise. Cloud chat is fine for public news. It is the wrong room for the file that moves the deal.

Size the unit by how many people ask in a busy hour, not by AUM. Hold M is the default for a 5–10 person shop. Same software on every size. Imaging is customized to the documents they will not paste.

A partner on a site visit should reach the same suite — not upload the IC deck to a lab from a hotel. Remote is access to their system. It is not our cloud.

Operators

Pricing is the business.

The spreadsheet they will not email. Source, customers, the sheet that is the company. If that file leaves, it is not “just a prompt.”

If they will not send it to a data company, they should not have to choose between no AI and someone else’s cloud. Plug in and go. Drop the file. Ask. Work stays in the room.

Someone will also ask from a phone. Same suite. Same files. Not a generic chatbot for the website, and not a lab tab because they left the building.

Not yet

Medical later.

Clinical operators may be a later fit. Paperwork comes first. This site makes no HIPAA claim and no treatment claim. If that is you, say so — we will not pretend we are ready.

Not a fit

If you need the smartest model on earth.

Use a frontier lab for that. Holdroom is for work that should stay in the room. Most serious firms should keep both until they trust it.
