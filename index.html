FAQ

Straight answers.

Does this replace ChatGPT?

For work you will not paste into ChatGPT, yes. For the hardest open-ended reasoning, the labs still win. Most firms keep both until they trust the box.

Where does the model come from?

Open-weight models that run on hardware you own. We preload and update them. You can turn updates off.

Can the whole firm use one box?

Yes. Seats are logins. Capacity is concurrent use. Hold M is built for a 5–10 person office that is not all asking at 9:05.

Are you shipping today?

Not yet. This page takes inquiries. If the fit is real, we will tell you when a unit can be imaged and delivered.
