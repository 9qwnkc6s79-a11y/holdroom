01 · Workspace

A familiar messaging workspace.

Pinned firm agents, file and photo attachments, voice dictation, routines, and a file room. On iPhone and on desktop. If you can message a colleague, you can use Hatch.

iPhone PWA

The same thread in your pocket.

Install Hatch on iPhone. Agents, files, and approvals stay in one conversation.

Desktop

Roster on the left. Work on the right.

Search, pin, and move between Hatch, Operations, Legal, and Finance without a new interface to learn.

Files & voice

Attach the note. Dictate the ask.

Drop a file or a photo into the composer. Speak when typing is the slower path.

02 · Connectors

Real workplace tools. You approve every write.

Built-in connections for Gmail, Outlook and Microsoft 365, Google Drive and Calendar, Slack, and Notion, plus IMAP/SMTP and custom MCP. Agents draft and prepare. Every outbound message or write pauses on an explicit Approve / Deny card.

Reads

Your mail, calendar, and files.

Hatch can look up what you already keep in those tools, inside your firm’s boundary.

Writes

Nothing leaves without you.

A send, a post, a page, or a calendar change waits on the card. Deny it and nothing is sent.

03 · Vault

An encrypted firm vault.

Logins, API keys, and payment cards stay encrypted inside your firm’s private boundary. Hatch can fill them into an authorized tool. The secret does not appear in the chat.

Encrypted Vault

Named. Never pasted.

  • Login — desk mailbox, referenced by name
  • Key — an API token the agent may use, not display
  • Card — last four only, after you approve the use

04 · Charter

Your open-weight model and ethics charter.

You set refusal posture, the worldview in the charter, communication style, and behavior. Open-weight models let your firm do that. Frontier labs will not. Zero firm data is sent to frontier AI APIs or to their training runs.

Posture

Strict, balanced, or direct.

The refusal line is yours. It is saved with the firm, not borrowed from a lab default.

Voice

How the answer should sound.

Concise, warm, blunt, or a formal memo. Length is a setting, not a surprise.

Weights

Open models on your boundary.

Ask and tools run on the models you choose. Firm text does not train someone else’s model.

05 · Deployment

Private hosted by default. Hardware if you need it.

Most firms start on dedicated infrastructure Hatch hosts for them. Firms that require physical custody of compute and data can put Hatch hardware in their own server room.

Default

Private dedicated cloud

Single-tenant Hatch infrastructure, hosted and managed for your firm.

  • Fast rollout, without a server room project.
  • Isolated encrypted logs.
  • A firm-controlled boundary. Not a shared frontier API.
Optional

On-site hardware

Dedicated Hatch hardware in your firm’s own server room.

  • Physical custody of compute and data.
  • Reached only over your existing VPN or WireGuard.
  • Same Hatch OS app. Same approvals. Same vault.
