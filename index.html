04 · Charter

Your open-weight model and ethics charter.

You set refusal posture, the worldview in the charter, communication style, and behavior. Open-weight models let your firm do that. Frontier labs will not. Zero firm data is sent to frontier AI APIs or to their training runs.

Posture

Strict, balanced, or direct.

The refusal line is yours. It is saved with the firm, not borrowed from a lab default.

Voice

How the answer should sound.

Concise, warm, blunt, or a formal memo. Length is a setting, not a surprise.

Weights

Open models on your boundary.

Ask and tools run on the models you choose. Firm text does not train someone else’s model.

05 · Deployment

Private hosted by default. Hardware if you need it.

Most firms start on dedicated infrastructure Hatch hosts for them. Firms that require physical custody of compute and data can put Hatch hardware in their own server room.

Default

Private Dedicated Cloud — Default

Single-tenant Hatch infrastructure, hosted and managed for your firm.

  • Fast rollout, without a server-room project.
  • Isolated encrypted logs.
  • A firm-controlled boundary. Not a shared frontier API.
Optional

Optional On-Site Hardware — Server Room over Your VPN

Dedicated Hatch hardware in your firm’s own server room, reached only over your VPN.

  • Physical custody of compute and data.
  • Reached exclusively over your existing VPN or WireGuard.
  • Same Hatch OS app, approvals, and vault.
