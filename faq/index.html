FAQ

Straight answers.

Short answers about the app, the boundary, and what we will not claim.

Is this a hardware box?
The product you use is Hatch OS. Private dedicated infrastructure is the default. On-site hardware is optional for firms that need the machines in their own server room.
Where does the text go?
Into your firm’s Hatch boundary. It is not sent to a frontier AI API, and it is not used to train one.
Can an agent send email on its own?
No. A send, a post, a page, or a calendar write stops on an Approve / Deny card. Deny means nothing was sent.
What is the vault?
Logins, API keys, and cards stored encrypted inside the boundary. An approved tool can be filled. The secret is not shown in the chat.
Do you have SOC 2, HIPAA, or ISO 27001?
No. We do not claim those. Ask us what the deployment actually does, and we will answer that.
Is there a public price?
No. Pricing is quoted for your firm. Start with Inquire or Request Beta Access.
