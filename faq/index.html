FAQ

Straight answers.

Short answers. If it’s not here, inquire.

Does this replace ChatGPT?
For work that should stay in the room, yes — that is the job. For the hardest open-ended reasoning, the labs still win this year. Most firms should keep both until they trust it.
Where does the model come from?
Open-weight models that run on premises — a chat model and a faster model, preloaded. We can offer signed updates. You can turn updates off. We do not claim they are as capable as a named frontier system.
Can the whole firm use one unit?
Yes. The owner signs in first, then invites seats. Capacity is concurrent use. Hold M is built for a 5–10 person office that is not all asking at 9:05. Firm software is $149/mo up to 8 seats. Same software on S, M, and L.
What is the difference between S, M, and L?
How many people can ask at once. S is 1–2 heavy chats ($1,999). M is 3–8 and the default ($3,749). L is 8–15 ($7,499). A larger unit does not make the model as smart as Claude or GPT. It lets more people use yours at the same time.
Are you shipping today?
Not yet. This site takes inquiries. If the fit is real, we will tell you when a unit can be imaged and delivered. North Texas built.
Do my files stay with me?
That is the design: work stays on premises, on your network. It does not go to the frontier labs or data companies. Outbound starts denied. A status / connections page shows whether traffic is leaving. We do not claim a certified air gap or that a person cannot walk the hardware out of the room.
Is this HIPAA or SOC 2?
No. We will not say HIPAA, SOC 2, ISO 27001, or “examiner-ready.” Medical is later, with paperwork first. See the security page.
Is this a kit I have to assemble?
No. It is a plug-and-go in-house suite: white-label mini workstation, imaged and encrypted, models loaded, firm app. A non-technical person should be able to drop a PDF and ask — no SSH. If you want a weekend Ollama build, you do not need us.
What does “customized to you” mean?
We confirm fit — busy hour, seats, the files you will not paste — then image the unit for that firm. Owner login, then invites. Documents you add stay on premises. It is not a generic public chatbot dropped on your network.
Can we use it from the road or a phone?
That is the intent: the same private suite — not a second AI in a lab — from the office, travel, or a phone. Remote means access to your system. The appliance still does not call a lab to answer. We will not invent a VPN product name or claim zero attack surface. How you get in from outside the office is confirmed when we image a unit, typically through remote access the firm already trusts. Pre-launch; we will be specific before you commit.
How does software billing work?
$29/mo for a solo login, or $149/mo for a firm up to 8 seats. No token bill. Hardware is a separate one-time purchase. Setup is quoted after we confirm fit.
Why not just buy a DGX Spark?
You can. NVIDIA’s public U.S. list is about $4,699 for serious local hardware. You still assemble the product: app, seats, watched folder, egress posture, and someone to call. Holdroom is the imaged workstation plus the firm app. Different job.
Do you have customers I can call?
Not ones we will invent for a website. Pre-launch. If you inquire, you talk to a person — not a fake logo wall.
How do documents get into the app?
Upload in the LAN app, or put files in a watched folder. The app can search those documents so people ask without sending the memo to a lab.
Where is the backup?
The encrypted disk you own — not a Holdroom cloud. Unplug the unit and the work stays with you.
