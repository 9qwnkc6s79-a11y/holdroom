FAQ

Straight answers.

Short answers. If it’s not here, inquire.

Does this replace ChatGPT?
For work that should stay in the room, yes — that is the job. For the hardest open-ended reasoning, the labs still win this year. Most firms should keep both until they trust it.
What is Hatch?
A private AI clean room for mid-size firms: an encrypted cluster in their environment, Hatch OS (login, files, remote over their VPN), a house model adapted on their data, plus a moral pack and a legal pack. Employees work without feeding OpenAI, Anthropic, or Bedrock.
Who is this for?
Hero market is 50–250 person firms. We design and sell Firm first. Team is an entry floor. Company and Campus appear on the price card only — they are not v1 build targets. Not consumers. Not giant enterprise.
What is the default quote?
Firm: $28k hardware + $48k/yr platform. Hardware is the encrypted cluster — how they believe us. Platform is the business: Hatch OS, packs, and the house model on their data. Team, Company, and Campus dollars are not published; talk to us.
Can the whole firm use one cluster?
That is the Firm job: one clean room for a mid-size office. The owner signs in first, then invites seats. Capacity is concurrent use. We confirm busy-hour fit before we image a unit.
Where does the model come from?
A house model adapted on the firm’s data, running on the cluster. We do not claim it is as capable as a named frontier system. We do not sell a Hatch cloud LLM as the product.
Are you shipping today?
Not yet. This site takes inquiries. If the fit is a Firm, we will tell you when a unit can be imaged and delivered. North Texas built.
Do my files stay with me?
That is the design: work stays on premises, on your network. It does not go to the frontier labs or data companies. Outbound starts denied. A status / connections page shows whether traffic is leaving. We do not claim a certified air gap or that a person cannot walk the hardware out of the room.
Is this HIPAA or SOC 2?
No. We will not say HIPAA, SOC 2, ISO 27001, or “examiner-ready.” Medical is later, with paperwork first. See the security page.
Is this a kit I have to assemble?
No. It is an imaged encrypted cluster plus Hatch OS. A non-technical person should be able to drop a PDF and ask — no SSH. If you want a weekend Ollama build, you do not need us.
What does “customized to you” mean?
We confirm fit — busy hour, seats, the files you will not paste — then image the cluster for that firm. Owner login, then invites. The house model is adapted on their data. It is not a generic public chatbot dropped on your network.
Can we use it from the road or a phone?
That is the intent: the same Hatch OS — not a second AI in a lab — from the office, travel, or a phone. Remote means access to your cluster over the firm’s VPN. The cluster still does not call a lab to answer. We will not invent a VPN product name or claim zero attack surface. Pre-launch; we will be specific before you commit.
How does billing work?
Firm default: $28k one-time hardware + $48k/yr platform. No token bill. Setup is quoted after we confirm fit. Other tiers are talk-to-us until those numbers are locked.
Why not just buy a local GPU kit?
You can. You still assemble the product: Hatch OS, seats, watched folder, packs, egress posture, and someone to call. Hatch is the imaged cluster plus the firm OS. Different job.
Do you have customers I can call?
Not ones we will invent for a website. Pre-launch. If you inquire, you talk to a person — not a fake logo wall.
How do documents get into Hatch OS?
Upload in the app, or put files in a watched folder. People ask without sending the memo to a lab.
Where is the backup?
The encrypted disk you own — not a Hatch cloud. Unplug the cluster and the work stays with you.
