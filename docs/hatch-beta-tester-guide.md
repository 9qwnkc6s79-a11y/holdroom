# Hatch beta — tester guide

You are trying Hatch before it is installed on a firm’s own hardware. This build runs the models on cloud test hardware (RunPod), not on a box in your office. Please don’t upload confidential client files.

Your tester invite code looks like `HATCH-XXXX`. That code is your sign-in. Don’t post it in a group chat.

## Install Hatch for iPhone (TestFlight)

1. Open your **TestFlight** invitation email or link on your iPhone.
2. If you do not already have Apple’s **TestFlight** app, tap **View in App Store** to install TestFlight first.
3. Return to the invite link (or tap **Redeem** in TestFlight) and tap **Install** next to **Hatch**.
4. Open **Hatch** from your Home Screen.

*(If you are testing on a desktop browser instead, use the preview link sent with your invite code.)*

## Sign in

1. Open **Hatch**.
2. Type your `HATCH-XXXX` invite code and tap **Continue**.
3. There is no second authenticator code. Your session stays signed in on your iPhone until you sign out or delete your account data in Profile.

## Please try these

1. **Ask about your connected email or Drive.** Open **General assistant** and ask a real question (for example, *"Can you get up to speed on..."*). You should see live progress above the composer (`Reading Gmail…` / `Reading Drive…`), followed by a concrete briefing rather than just subject lines.
2. **Connect apps & browse Library.** Tap the grid button -> **Connect apps** to connect Gmail / Google Drive / Calendar, then open **Library** to search synced items and firm files. Tap any item to read it or ask about it in chat.
3. **Talk across threads.** Send two or three messages, start a **Fresh chat** from the `...` menu, and switch back between recent threads. Leave the app and come back — your threads persist on the host.
4. **Attach a photo or document.** Tap `+` in the composer to attach a photo or sample note.
5. **Try voice.** Tap the blue orb button in the composer for voice mode, or the microphone button to dictate.
6. **Model & Ethics + Profile.** Open your avatar -> **Model & Ethics** to switch between Hermes (tool calling) and Qwen, adjust tone/charter, and review **Privacy Policy**, **Terms of Service**, or **Delete account & data** in Profile.
