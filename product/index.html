Product

Ontology, then the rest of the stack.

Hatch is not a smarter public model. It is an operating layer: encrypted cluster, Hatch OS, an ontology over your knowledge base, then chat and packs on top. Customized to the firm. Remote over your VPN. Not a Hatch cloud LLM.

The stack

Five layers. Ontology in the middle.

Chat is one interface. The system is the object model, the knowledge base, and the permissions under it.

Ontology

The operating layer.

The ontology is how Hatch names the firm’s world and the links between things. Retrieval, permissions, and chat all hang off that model.

We image it to how the firm already works. It is not a generic public chatbot dropped on the LAN, and it is not “chat on docs” with a new label. Pre-launch: exact object types are confirmed when we talk about fit.

What we mean

Objects, then interfaces

  • A shared model of the work — not a prompt per person
  • Relations the firm already uses (who, what file, which matter)
  • Permissions on the object, not only on a folder
  • Chat, search, and packs as applications on top
  • Customized at imaging. Not a one-size public graph.

Knowledge base

Customized to their corpus.

The pass test is still simple: a non-technical person drops a PDF and asks. The difference is the file lands in a knowledge base the ontology can use — not a one-off chat attachment.

Confirm the corpus

The files they will not paste. Memos, decks, sheets, source. We confirm fit before we image a unit.

Upload and watched folder

Drop a file in Hatch OS, or put the set in a folder the cluster watches. People are not re-uploading every morning.

House model on their data

Private tenant inference on the firm’s files. Work does not feed OpenAI, Anthropic, Bedrock, or a shared Hatch training set.

Under the ontology

Cluster, OS, network.

L0 · Hardware

Encrypted cluster

Firm-owned, in their environment. Imaged before it ships. Encrypted disk. It looks like office equipment, not a science project.

L1 · Hatch OS

Login, files, their VPN

ChatGPT-like login. Seats the owner invites. Same OS from the road — over the firm’s VPN, not a Hatch cloud.

Network

Default-deny outbound

The cluster does not call a lab to answer a question. Outbound starts off. Optional signed updates are a choice you can disable.

Remote, plainly

Over their VPN.

A partner on a flight should reach the same operating layer — not open a lab tab because they left the office. Files still stay with you.

Remote is access to Hatch OS on the firm’s cluster, typically through the VPN the firm already trusts. We will not name a VPN product or claim zero attack surface. Pre-launch. We will be specific before you commit.
