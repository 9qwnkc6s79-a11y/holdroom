Documents

Upload, a watched folder, then ask.

The pass test is simple: a non-technical person drops a PDF and asks a question. No SSH. No weekend build.

Upload

In Hatch OS, drop a memo, deck, or spreadsheet. Ask about it in the same place.

Watched folder

Put files in a folder the cluster watches. People are not re-uploading the same set every morning.

Ask from the room

Remote, plainly

Over their VPN.

A partner on a flight should reach the same clean room — not open a lab tab because they left the office. Files still stay with you.

Remote is access to Hatch OS on the firm’s cluster, typically through the VPN the firm already trusts. We will not name a VPN product or claim zero attack surface. Pre-launch. We will be specific before you commit.
