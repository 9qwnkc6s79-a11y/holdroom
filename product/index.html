03 Product

A plug-and-go in-house suite.

The full system on your network: workstation, models, and the firm app, imaged before it ships. Secure data stays with you. Customized to how you work. Same private AI from the office or the road. Not a smarter model than the labs. Not a kit. Pre-launch inquiries only.

The object

Ready. Same software on every size.

Hold S, M, and L are the same suite with more concurrent room. We size by how many people ask in a busy hour — not by job title.

Hardware

White-label mini workstation

Imaged before it ships. Encrypted disk. Compute, memory, and local models already on the unit. It looks like office equipment, not a science project.

Models

Open-weight, preloaded

A chat model for longer work and a faster model for quick asks. Both run on premises. We can offer signed updates. You can turn updates off.

The app

One place for the firm

Chat. Drop a file. Or put documents in a watched folder. The app retrieves from those documents so people can ask without pasting into a lab.

People

Owner first, then seats

The owner signs in first. Then they invite seats. Solo software is one login. Firm software is up to 8 seats. Capacity is still concurrent use — that is why S, M, and L exist.

Customized

Imaged for this firm

Size, seats, and the documents you will not paste. We confirm fit before we image. This is not a generic public chatbot dropped on your LAN.

Remote

Same suite from the road

Travel, phone, anywhere you already work. Access to your private system — not a Holdroom cloud and not a lab. Exact path confirmed at imaging. We will not name a VPN product or claim zero attack surface.

Network

Default-deny outbound

The unit does not call a lab to answer a question. Outbound starts off. Optional signed updates are a choice you can disable. Remote access does not change that posture.

Backup

The encrypted disk you own

There is no Holdroom cloud copy. Backup is the disk in the room — or a copy you make of that disk. If you unplug it, the work stays with you.

Documents

Upload, a watched folder, then ask.

The pass test is simple: a non-technical person drops a PDF and asks a question. No SSH. No weekend build.

Upload

In the app, drop a memo, deck, or spreadsheet. Ask about it in the same place.

Watched folder

Put files in a folder the unit watches. The app can search those documents so the firm is not re-uploading the same set every morning.

Ask from the room

Retrieval stays on premises. The answer is generated on the unit. The document does not go to a frontier lab or a data company.

Remote, plainly

The building is the firm.

A partner on a flight, or someone on a phone, should reach the same private suite — not open a lab tab because they left the office. Files still stay with you.

How you get in from outside the office is confirmed when we image a unit, typically through remote access the firm already trusts for the rest of its work. Pre-launch. We will be specific before you commit. The appliance still does not call a lab to answer.
