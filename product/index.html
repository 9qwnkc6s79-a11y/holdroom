Product

What you actually get.

A white-label mini workstation you own, imaged before it ships. Work stays on your network. Your files stay yours — they do not go to the frontier labs or data companies. Not a smarter model than the labs. Not a kit. Pre-launch inquiries only.

The object

A ready appliance. Same software on every size.

Hold S, M, and L are the same product with more concurrent room. We size by how many people ask in a busy hour — not by job title.

Hardware

White-label mini workstation

Imaged before it ships. Encrypted disk. Compute, memory, and local models already on the unit. It looks like office equipment, not a science project.

Models

Open-weight, preloaded

A chat model for longer work and a faster model for quick asks. Both run on premises. We can offer signed updates. You can turn updates off.

The app

One place on your LAN

An intranet app on your network. Chat. Drop a file. Or put documents in a watched folder. The app retrieves from those documents so people can ask without pasting into a lab.

People

Owner first, then seats

The owner signs in first. Then they invite seats. Solo software is one login. Firm software is up to 8 seats. Capacity is still concurrent use — that is why S, M, and L exist.

Network

Default-deny outbound

The unit does not call a lab to answer a question. Outbound starts off. Optional signed updates are a choice you can disable. A status / connections page shows whether traffic is leaving.

Backup

The encrypted disk you own

There is no Holdroom cloud copy. Backup is the disk in the room — or a copy you make of that disk. If you unplug it, the work stays with you.

Documents

Upload, a watched folder, then ask.

The pass test is simple: a non-technical person drops a PDF and asks a question. No SSH. No weekend build.

Upload

In the LAN app, drop a memo, deck, or spreadsheet. Ask about it in the same place.

Watched folder

Put files in a folder the unit watches. The app can search those documents so the firm is not re-uploading the same set every morning.

Ask from the room

Retrieval stays on premises. The answer is generated on the unit. The document does not go to a frontier lab or a data company.
