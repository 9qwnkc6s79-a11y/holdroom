Label: DEMO. Two paths. Do not invent point values beyond what is written here.

Guests use the Boundaries Coffee LLC Rewards Program on TapMango.

Published DEMO tiers (as on the current TapMango card):
- 500 points — Free house drip coffee
- 1000 points — Free small specialty drink

- 2000 points — Free Boundaries mug

Staff: ask “are you on TapMango?” before ringing. Do not stack a text punch on top of a TapMango redeem in the same visit unless Ops says so.
