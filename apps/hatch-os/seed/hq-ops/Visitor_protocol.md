# Meridian Advisory — Visitor protocol

Sample firm operations note for the Hatch beta.

Operations and desk leads use this checklist when a client or advisor group visits on site.

1. Confirm the date, headcount, and host desk (North desk or South desk).
2. Reserve the conference room on the Operations calendar.
3. Share the arrival instructions with the visitor ahead of time.

- Groups over 20 people require two days' notice.
- Keep desk-specific materials inside that desk's file room unless shared through Operations.
