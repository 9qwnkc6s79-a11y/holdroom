# DEMO — Boundaries Coffee catering protocol

Label: DEMO. Not a live Toast feed. No prices invented here — quote from the current catering sheet at HQ.

## Who this is for
HQ / Ops and store leads. Use this when a church, office, or school asks for coffee service.

## How a request comes in
1. Guest texts or emails Info@boundariescoffee.com or the store inbox.
2. HQ logs the event: date, window, headcount, drip vs specialty, delivery vs pickup.
3. Confirm only after a lead (Daniel / Ops) signs the order. Stores do not accept a large catering hold without HQ.

## Timing
- 48 hours notice preferred for 20+ drinks.
- Same-day: drip boxes and brew-bar only if the store GM says the batch board can take it.
- Sunday church windows: lock brew start 90 minutes before pickup.

## What we send
- Cambro or boxed drip, cups, lids, sugars, dairy, oat, a simple card with brew time.
- Specialty trays (lattes) only when a barista can leave the window — usually Prosper Saturday or Little Elm weekday mid-morning.

## Day-of
GM on duty (Rafael at Little Elm, Heath at Prosper) checks the board at open. If the order is for the other store, do not pull that store’s beans or cups — switch rooms / call HQ.

## After
Return cambros the next business morning. Note waste on the DEMO catering log — do not invent dollar amounts.
