# DEMO — Meridian Advisory visitor protocol

Label: DEMO. Sample firm notes for the beta. Not a client file.

Operations and the desk leads use this when a visitor group asks for a working session on site.

1. The guest emails the sample inbox or the desk.
2. Confirm headcount, date, and which desk (North or South).
3. Hold the room on the operations calendar.
4. Send a one-page overview. Do not invent fees.

- Groups over 20 people need two days' notice.
- A specialist session only when someone from that desk can host it.

The lead on duty checks the board at open. If the visit is for the other desk, do not use that desk's materials. Hand it to Operations.
