# Engagement notes

Counsel keeps matter notes in this file room. Do not copy them into Operations or Finance.

- New engagements get a one-page note: who asked, what was asked, and what was not asked.
- Do not invent a legal conclusion. Flag the question and leave the answer for a person.
- Outside counsel names stay in this room.

This is a firm note for the Legal agent. It is not legal advice.
