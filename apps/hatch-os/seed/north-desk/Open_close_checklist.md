# North desk — Daily open / close checklist

Desk: Meridian Advisory — North desk

## Open

1. Review the Operations calendar for scheduled client visits.
2. Confirm conference rooms and guest packets are ready.
3. Log the desk open time with Operations.

## Close

1. Secure printed matter files in locked cabinets.
2. Confirm any pending handoffs are logged for Operations.
3. Note "North desk closed" on the daily log.
