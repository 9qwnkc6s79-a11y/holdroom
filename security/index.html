Security

The data stays with you.

Work stays on premises, on your network. It does not go to the frontier labs or data companies. We will not dress that up as a certification we do not have.

We will say

What is true today

  • Your files stay yours. Prompts and outputs stay on premises.
  • Work does not go to a frontier lab or a data company to answer a question.
  • Outbound starts denied. Signed updates can be disabled.
  • Disk encryption is on before the unit ships.
  • A local status / connections page shows model, disk, and whether traffic is leaving.
  • Backup is the encrypted disk you own — not our cloud. You can unplug the unit.
We will not say

Lines we will not cross

  • HIPAA compliant, HIPAA ready, or BAA language.
  • SOC 2, ISO 27001, or “examiner-ready.”
  • Zero risk. Zero egress as a certified fact.
  • Beats Claude, GPT, or any named frontier model.
  • Named customers, case studies, or revenue we do not have.
  • Medical or legal advice. We sell an appliance and an app.

Threat model, simply

This solves the paste problem. Not every problem.

It is for

An associate who would otherwise send a client memo to a lab. A deal team that will not give the IC deck to a data company. An operator who will not put the pricing sheet in someone else’s cloud.

It is not for

Someone who walks the unit out of the room. A compromised laptop on the same LAN. A firm that needs a signed audit letter this quarter. Those are different products and different paperwork.

Physical reality

If someone has the hardware, they have the hardware. Encryption helps. Policies help. We will not pretend a marketing site is your incident plan.

Medical

Later. Paperwork first.

We may serve clinical operators later. Until then this site makes no HIPAA claim, no treatment claim, and no “hospital-ready” claim. If that is your need, say so on the inquiry — we will be honest about timing.
