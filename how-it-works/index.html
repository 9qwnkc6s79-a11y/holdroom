How it works

Open the thread. Approve the write.

You sign in to Hatch OS. You message an agent the way you message a colleague. The agent reads your files and tools. It does not send, post, or change anything until you approve the card.

1

Sign in on iPhone or desktop.

The app is the product. Your team does not learn a new console.

2

Connect the tools you already use.

Mail, calendar, Drive, Slack, Notion, IMAP, or an MCP server you control.

3

Ask in the thread.

Attach a file, dictate, or point at the file room. The answer cites what it used.

4

Approve or deny.

Outbound mail, posts, pages, and calendar writes stop on the card. Deny means nothing was sent.
