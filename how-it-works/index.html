How it works

Unbox. Plug in. Ask.

The job is the document they refused to paste. The path is short on purpose. The data stays with you — it does not go to the frontier labs.

1

Unbox

A white-label mini workstation, already imaged. Disk encrypted. Chat and fast models loaded. You do not assemble a stack.

2

Plug in

Ethernet to your network. Power on. The owner signs in first on the LAN app, then invites seats. No public cloud login. No SSH to ask a question.

3

Ask

Drop a PDF, or put files in the watched folder. Ask. Retrieval and answers stay on premises. Your files stay yours.

1

Arrive imaged

The unit ships ready. Encryption is on. Models are local. Same software whether you ordered S, M, or L.

2

Join the room’s network

Plug into the LAN. People reach one intranet app. Work stays on your network — not a lab account.

3

Owner login, then invites

The first account is the owner. They invite seats. Solo is one login; firm software is up to 8 seats.

4

Add documents

Upload in the app, or drop files into a watched folder. The app can search those documents so a question can use the memo that should stay in the room.

5

Ask without leaving the floor

Chat with the local models. The pass test: a non-technical person drops a PDF and asks. No SSH.

6

Check the status / connections page

See model state, disk encryption, and whether outbound is on. Proof for IT and counsel — not a sales paragraph.

Network

Default-deny outbound.

The unit does not call a frontier lab or a data company to answer. Egress starts off. Signed updates are optional and can be disabled. If IT wants a hole, that is a written choice — not a silent default.

Backup and proof

Backup

There is no Holdroom cloud. Backup is the encrypted disk you own, or a copy you make of it. Unplug the unit and the work stays with you.

What the status page shows

Models loaded, disk lock, outbound on/off, update checks on/off. Plain language. Screenshot-friendly for IT.

What it is not

Not a public uptime badge. Not a SOC 2 report. Not a claim that nothing can leak if someone walks the hardware out of the room.
