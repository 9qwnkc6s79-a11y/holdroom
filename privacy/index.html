Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Who we are. Hatch Systems (“Hatch”, “we”) makes Hatch OS, the Hatch OS iPhone app, and the website hatchsystems.ai. To contact us about privacy or your data, use the inquiry form on hatchsystems.ai.

1. Scope: two very different settings

A. Hatch Beta and the Hatch-hosted beta server. During the beta, the app connects to a server Hatch operates on cloud hosting (currently Vercel), and AI models run on rented cloud GPUs (currently RunPod). Hatch is responsible for this data.

B. Customer deployments (on-premises Hatch hardware). For firms that deploy Hatch on-premises, Hatch OS runs on hardware at the firm and is reached over the firm’s own VPN or WireGuard. The firm controls its data and decides how it is used. Hatch acts on the firm’s instructions (as a service provider / processor) and normally does not hold the firm’s files. The firm’s own privacy notice governs its users.

The rest of this policy covers setting A, the website, and anything Hatch itself handles in setting B (such as support requests and license or billing data).

3. How we use it

  • To provide the service: sign-in, chat, search your files, run agents and connectors, and show usage and status.
  • To secure it: logs, audit trails, and abuse prevention.
  • To fix and improve the beta: feedback, crash reports, and diagnostics during the beta.
  • To communicate with you about the beta or your inquiry.
  • We do not use your content or connected-app data to train or fine-tune shared models without separate, explicit written consent. For customer deployments, any firm-specific model adaptation happens on the firm’s own hardware, under the firm’s agreement.
  • We do not send your content to frontier AI API providers (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, or xAI) for inference.

Google user data. Hatch’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We use Gmail, Drive, and Calendar data only to provide the features you request, do not use it for advertising, and do not let humans read it except with your consent, for security, or as the law requires.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Beta content: kept while the beta runs, then deleted or de-identified within a reasonable period after it ends, unless you ask for earlier deletion.
  • Audit and security logs: retained as needed for security, abuse prevention, and service operation.
  • Website inquiries: retained as needed to respond to your inquiry and manage beta access.
  • How to delete: use Settings > Account in the web app (or Profile > Delete account and data in the iPhone app), or submit a deletion request via our inquiry form. We will confirm once deletion is complete.
  • Revoking consent: you can disconnect a Google connector in the app at any time, and revoke Hatch’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can stop using the beta and ask for deletion at any time.

6. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), and connector tokens and vault items are stored encrypted. No system is perfectly secure. Hatch does not claim HIPAA, SOC 2, or ISO 27001 compliance.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your data, or to object to some processing. Contact us through the inquiry form on hatchsystems.ai. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

8. Children

Hatch is for professional use and is not directed to children under 13 (or the age required locally). We do not knowingly collect children’s data.

9. Changes

We will post changes here and update the date above. If a change is material, we will notify you in the app or by email where available.
